# Sales Performance & Business Dashboard

An Excel-based Sales Performance & Business Dashboard built to clean, analyze, visualize, and derive business insights from 500 sales transactions.

##  Project Overview

This project analyzes sales performance across products, categories, states, payment modes, months, and discount levels.

The objective is to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights using Microsoft Excel and an interactive dashboard.

##  Business Problem

Businesses need to understand:

- Overall sales and profit performance
- Which categories and products generate the most revenue
- Which states contribute the most sales
- Monthly sales trends
- Customer payment preferences
- The relationship between discounts and profitability

This dashboard provides a centralized view of these business metrics.

##  Data Cleaning

The raw dataset was cleaned before analysis.

Major cleaning steps included:

- Handling missing Customer Name values
- Reviewing missing Sales values
- Checking duplicate Order IDs
- Removing extra spaces from text fields
- Checking category consistency
- Standardizing Order Date format
- Formatting Sales and Profit as currency
- Formatting Discount as percentage
- Formatting Quantity as a whole number
- Creating a structured Excel Table named `SalesData`

##  Key Insights

- Total Sales: **₹17,557,936.49**
- Total Profit: **₹3,231,398.32**
- Total Orders: **500**
- Total Quantity: **1,533**
- Average Order Value: **₹35,115.87**
- Profit Margin: **18.40%**

### Category Insight

Electronics generated the highest sales and profit among the four categories.

### Product Insight

Laptop was the highest-selling product by sales.

### State Insight

Maharashtra generated the highest sales and profit among the analyzed states.

### Payment Insight

Net Banking recorded the highest number of orders, while Cash generated the highest sales among the payment modes.

### Discount Insight

The 25% discount level contains only one order, so it should not be used alone to make a general conclusion about discount performance.

##  Business Recommendations

- Monitor inventory and availability of high-performing electronics products.
- Focus on high-performing products such as laptops, smartphones, tablets, printers, and monitors.
- Closely monitor the Maharashtra market because of its strong sales and profit contribution.
- Evaluate discount strategies using both sales and profitability rather than sales alone.
- Continue monitoring payment-mode performance and customer transaction behavior.
